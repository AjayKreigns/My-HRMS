iFluids HRMS

People, pay and time, together.

Overview

iFluids HRMS is the internal people, pay and time system of iFluids Engineering, a process safety and HSE consulting partnership. It is a work tool: calm, dense and scannable, built for HR staff clearing queues and employees finishing a task in under a minute.

Content fundamentals

  • Voice. Plain and direct, second person to the employee ("Your request was submitted", "You're all caught up."). Say what happened or what will: "Leave applied — waiting for approval", "Employee added with an onboarding checklist".
  • Casing. Sentence case everywhere except module names ("Manage Employees", "My Finances", "Expenses & Travel") and the uppercase micro-labels set by table-head and nav-group.
  • Buttons start with the verb and name the object: "Apply leave", "Raise ticket", "Submit declaration", "Mark all read". No "Click here", no exclamation marks.
  • Errors say what is wrong and how to fix it: "The amount cannot exceed three months of salary.", "Reason is required." Never apologise, never show codes.
  • Names. The company is "iFluids Engineering", short name "iFluids"; never add "Pvt. Ltd." (it is a partnership firm). The product is "iFluids HRMS". Dates read "09 Nov 2026"; money reads "₹12,40,000" with Indian grouping; periods read "FY 2026-27".
  • No emoji. Status is a word in a Badge; icons are the stroke set below.

Visual foundations

  • Colour. Deep navy (side, ink) and teal (brand) carry the identity; coral (accent) is a small signal, used for notification dots and the Inbox count only. State uses green, amber, red and blue, shown as Badge pairs, never as bare coloured text on white. Cards (surface) sit on the page ground (bg); panel is one step off surface for table headers and modal feet. Light is the default theme; dark deepens the same navy and teal (bg #0a1420, surface #101d2c, brand lightens to #14a394).
  • Type. Plus Jakarta Sans only, weights 400 to 800, 13px base. Headings are bold with slightly tight tracking (page-title 24px, -0.02em). Numbers that line up use tabular numerals (stat-value, tables, clocks). Micro-labels are uppercase with wide tracking (table-head, nav-group).
  • Spacing and layout. Steps of 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 and 30px (space-1 to space-6). Cards pad 20px, grids gap 20px, and the page head sits 22px above content. The page is a 240px navy rail plus a 64px top bar and a content column; below 768px the rail becomes a drawer.
  • Shape. Radii 8 (inputs), 10 (buttons), 14 (cards), 18 (modals), pill (badges). Borders are 1px line; depth is shadow-card at rest and shadow-pop for dialogs and menus. Over the page's soft colour wash (teal, blue, coral and violet radials at low alpha) cards and menus become frosted glass (glass, glass-strong, glass-line).
  • Motion. Springy and short: cards and modals rise 16px and scale up from 94% over 0.3 to 0.5s with a long settle; the backdrop fades in over 0.3s; up to six siblings stagger by about 0.04s. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
  • States. Hover on ghost buttons turns border and text brand; hover on primary darkens to brand-dark; focus is a brand border with a 3px translucent ring on fields and a 2px brand outline on buttons; disabled is 50% opacity.
  • Contrast. Body and heading text meet 4.5:1 in both themes. Known misses, kept as in the source: muted on bg or panel in light (4.4:1), white on green (3.3:1), white on brand in dark (3.1:1), white initials on the lighter avatar colours. Use the matching badge-*-fg pairs instead of green or amber as text.

Iconography

Inline SVG stroke icons on a 24px grid at stroke width 1.8, round caps and joins, currentColor, shown at 14 to 18px (icon). The set (home, inbox, team, wallet, org, chart, settings, clock, calendar, plane, briefcase, help, book, search, bell, plus, edit, check, x, upload, download, chevrons, star, message, file, laptop, award, target, menu, refresh, money and more) lives in app/src/ui.jsx as one path per name. Icons sit before a label with an 8 to 11px gap; icon-only controls use icon-btn with an aria-label. No filled icons and no emoji. The previews here redraw only the few icons they show.

Assets

The iFluids Engineering logo (assets/Logos/ifluids-logo.png, 143 x 50) always sits on a white plate (white fill, 9px radius, 3px 6px padding), including on the navy sidebar. Don't recolour or redraw it.

Components

Each preview is live HTML using the classes below it. Copy the markup and keep the class names.

Button

Use a button for one action. The page's main action is btn btn-primary (one per view, top right of the page head or the dialog's Save). Everything else is btn-ghost. btn-danger is for clock out and destructive confirmations; btn-success only for Approve in approval rows, paired with a ghost Reject.

The consumer provides the label (verb first: "Apply leave", "Raise ticket", "Mark all read") and an optional 14px stroke icon before it. Standard height is 38px (radius-md); inside tables and cards use btn-sm (30px, radius-sm). Icon-only actions use icon-btn with an aria-label.

Do: keep one primary per view; show a toast after the click ("Leave applied", "Comp off requested"). Don't: put white text on green at 12px (3.3:1); use btn-sm for the page-level action; stack two primaries.

A number tile for the top of an admin or list page: stat-value (26px bold, tabular numerals), stat-label (12.5px muted) and an optional stat-sub. The 4px left stripe carries state: stat-brand for neutral counts, stat-green good, stat-amber needs attention, stat-red problem. Green and amber values use the dark badge-*-fg tones so they stay readable on surface.

Show at most four in a row, each with a label that names what is counted. Use them where the counts are the first thing to scan (Manage Employees, Helpdesk, Hiring, Reports), not on every page.

Badge

ApprovedPendingRejectedSubmittedNotice periodDraft

A pill that states a record's status in a word. Map status to tone: green = Approved, Paid, Active, Completed, Resolved; amber = Pending, Probation, Behind, Manager review pending; red = Rejected, Absent, Overdue; blue = Submitted, Scheduled, In progress, Open; purple = Notice period, Offer; grey = Draft, Closed, Cancelled, Withdrawn.

Always keep the word; colour alone never carries state. Fill and text come in matched pairs (badge-<tone>-bg with badge-<tone>-fg, 4.7:1 or better). The source keeps these light fills in the dark theme too.

Tabs

Switches views inside one page ("Leave requests / Balances / Leave types / Holidays"). The bar is a line-soft track with a 12px radius; the active tab lifts to a surface chip with a light shadow, and inactive tabs are muted bold 13px. The track scrolls sideways on narrow screens.

The consumer provides short noun labels and, where useful, a count in brackets ("Requests (3)"). Use tabs for views of the same object, never for steps. Put them directly under the page head with 20px below. Use role="tab" and aria-selected.

Field

Your manager sees this with the request.

A labelled control: label (12px semibold, label colour) above an input, select or textarea at least 40px tall, input-border 1px border, radius-sm. Focus changes the border to brand and adds a 3px translucent brand ring. Required fields add a red star (req) after the label; optional ones say "(optional)" in the label.

The consumer provides the label and the value, and in a form modal a two-column grid (full spans both). Put the error sentence at the form bottom ("The amount cannot exceed three months of salary."), saying what to change. Don't use placeholder text as the label.

EmployeeChip

An avatar with the person's name and a one-line role. The avatar is a circle with white initials (first letters of first and last name, font size 38% of the diameter, semibold) on a colour assigned to each employee from a ten-colour palette: #5746af, #0d9488, #d97706, #2563eb, #db2777, #16a34a, #7c3aed, #dc2626, #0891b2, #65a30d. The consumer provides the person's name, initials and avatar colour. Always show the name next to the avatar in lists; avatars alone are fine only in a compact row of people. Unknown person: show an em dash. Note that white initials on the lighter palette colours (amber, green, lime) fall under 4.5:1; keep initials at 11px bold or larger and always pair with the name.

Progress

A 6px bar on a line-soft track with a brand fill (or green at 100%). Use it for goal progress and onboarding checklists, with the figure beside it ("3 of 5 done"). Never use it without a number or label nearby; the bar alone doesn't state a value.

Modal

Raise a ticket

A frosted dialog over a 50% navy backdrop with a 3px blur: glass-strong fill, glass-line border, radius-xl (18px), shadow-pop. It has a head (modal-title and a close icon button), a body (22px padding) and a panel foot with Cancel (ghost) then the primary action, right aligned. Width 620px; use 920px for tables.

The consumer provides the title, the body and the actions. Render it into the page body (the app uses a portal), never inside an animated page container, or the overlay is clipped to that container. It sits 56px from the top, scrolls inside the backdrop, closes on Escape, on a click outside and on the close button, and carries role="dialog" and aria-modal.

Toast

Leave applied — waiting for approval
The selected dates fall on weekly offs or holidays.

A short confirmation after an action, fixed bottom right (16px from the edges, 8px apart): ink fill with surface text, radius-md, max 320px. toast-error uses red with white text. State what happened in the past tense or as a result ("Comp off requested", "Employee added with an onboarding checklist"). Don't use a toast for something the person must act on; use an inline message.
