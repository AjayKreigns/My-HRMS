# Scans run on 2026-11-27

Commit `2a5ad03` plus uncommitted changes (the three QA fixes, the Playwright setup). All scans were read only, against the local app with the README demo accounts, unless noted.

## Accessibility

Tool: axe-core 4.14.1, rule tags WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA plus best practice, run through Playwright on 30 pages (login, 28 sidebar routes as admin, the payslip dialog). All 30 pages had at least one finding. 4 WCAG rules and 6 best-practice rules were violated.

| Impact | Rule | Standard | Pages | Elements | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| serious | color-contrast | WCAG 1.4.3 AA | 30 | 355 | `<button class="lg-btn">Login</button>` |
| serious | label-content-name-mismatch | WCAG 2.5.3 | 29 | 59 | `<a class="nav-item" aria-label="Inbox">` (visible text differs) |
| serious | aria-progressbar-name | WCAG 1.1.1 | 1 | 1 | `.day-bar-track` with `role="progressbar"` and no name |
| serious | aria-prohibited-attr | WCAG 4.1.2 | 1 | 4 | `<g class="lv-ring" aria-label="Casual Leave: 6 of 11 days left">` |
| moderate | heading-order | best practice | 18 | 18 | `<h3>Quick actions</h3>` with no h2 before it |
| moderate | region | best practice | 1 | 9 | `.lg-art` outside any landmark |
| moderate | page-has-heading-one | best practice | 1 | 1 | page has no h1 |
| moderate | landmark-no-duplicate-banner | best practice | 1 | 1 | `<header class="topbar">` |
| moderate | landmark-unique | best practice | 1 | 1 | `<header class="topbar">` |
| minor | empty-table-header | best practice | 10 | 10 | `<th></th>` |

Keyboard check on the login page (first 9 Tab stops): email, password, Login, "Forgot password ?", the two store badges, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, then back to the page. Every stop showed a focus ring.

Not covered: screen reader testing, dark theme, zoom and reflow, form error announcements, pages reached only through clicks (dialogs other than the payslip).

## Dependency audit

`npm audit`, 4 issues.

| Package | Severity | Direct | Used in | Fix offered |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| jspdf 2.5.2 | critical (12 advisories) | yes | payslip PDF | jspdf 4.2.1 (breaking) |
| dompurify 2.5.9 | moderate | via jspdf | jsPDF's html feature | comes with the jspdf upgrade |
| vite 5.4.21 | high | yes | dev server and build | vite 8.4.0 (breaking) |
| esbuild | moderate | via vite | dev server | comes with the vite upgrade |

The app calls only `text`, `line`, `rect`, `addImage`, `setFont`, `setFontSize`, `setTextColor`, `setFillColor` and `save` on jsPDF, so the named jsPDF advisories (new-window output, addJS, AcroForm, file loading) look unreachable. jsPDF's own code was not read. The vite and esbuild issues affect the development server only.
